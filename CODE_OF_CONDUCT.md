# Code of conduct

delune follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short:

- Be kind and patient. People come to this project with different experience,
  languages and backgrounds.
- Keep criticism about the work, and make it specific enough to act on.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, or publishing anyone's
  private information.
- Don't use issues or discussions to ask for, share or point to copyrighted music.
  delune is for music you have the right to get.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour is a problem, report it privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/PndaMan/delune/security/advisories/new)
(choose it even though it isn't a security issue), or by contacting
[@PndaMan](https://github.com/PndaMan) on GitHub. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, and block people, whose behaviour
goes against this code, in this repository and in the project's other spaces.
