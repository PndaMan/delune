# On your phone

The web app is built for phones first.

## Install it

- **iPhone and iPad:** open delune in Safari → **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** open it in Chrome → **⋮** → **Install app**.

It opens full screen, with shortcuts (long-press the icon) for **Search**,
**Wishlist** and **Review**. On Android it shows up in the share sheet: share a
Spotify, Apple Music or Bandcamp link to delune to search for it.

HTTPS is needed to install it — use your [reverse proxy](../connect/reverse-proxy.md)
or [Tailscale](../connect/remote.md).

## Around the app

- The bottom bar has **Search, Downloads, Review, Soulseek** (admins) and **Settings**.
  It steps aside while you type.
- Releases, albums and wishlists open as sheets from the bottom.
- The Review tab shows how many albums are waiting.
- Live updates keep every screen current without refreshing.
