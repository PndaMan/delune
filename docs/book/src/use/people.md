# People, permissions and requests

## Accounts

Everyone signs in with their Navidrome account; delune keeps no passwords of its own.
Navidrome admins are delune admins, and that follows Navidrome within seconds.
Sessions last 30 days from last use. Settings → Your account lists your devices, and
can sign the others out.

## Permissions

Settings → People (admins) lists everyone who has signed in. Each person has:

| Permission | Allows |
|---|---|
| **Search** | Searching Soulseek, opening releases, browsing people |
| **Download** | Starting downloads, the wishlist, following artists |
| **Request** | Asking for albums for someone to approve |
| **Skip approval** | Importing their own downloads when imports need approval |
| **Manage** | Everyone's downloads and requests, people, and all settings |

New people can search, download and request. **Imports need approval** (a switch on the
same page) makes members' downloads wait for an admin.

Members don't see the Soulseek tab (the account, chat, rooms and uploads) or the
Library and imports settings.

## Requests

People who can't download (or would rather ask) press **Request** on a release, or ask
for an album by name. Admins, and anyone who can manage, see requests on the Downloads
page and **approve** or **decline** them. An approved request downloads, and the
requester follows it through: pending, approved, downloading, ready for review, in the
library, or declined — with a notification at each step that matters.

## Notifications

The bell collects what you need to know: a request decided, a download ready or
failed, an album added to the library. For admins: new requests.
