# Soulseek: people, favourites, chat, sharing

The **Soulseek** tab (admins) is everything about the account beyond search.

## People

Look someone up by name to see their profile, picture, speed, queue, and **everything
they share**, as a folder tree you can filter. Any folder opens like a search result,
ready to download.

### Favourites

Press the **star** on someone's profile to make them a favourite. delune then keeps
their share list saved, so their shares open instantly next time instead of waiting on
their connection, and quietly refreshes it: when you open them (if it's older than ten
minutes) and every six hours. Favourites are listed on the People tab, per person.

## Messages and rooms

Private messages with other users, and public chat rooms: join, read and talk. delune
rejoins your rooms when it restarts.

## Uploads

What people are downloading from you, with the option to cancel. Sharing itself is set
up in [Settings → Sharing](../connect/soulseek.md#sharing).

## Stats

The top of the People tab shows what you share, uploads and downloads running, and the
totals transferred, kept across restarts.

## Hiding people

**Hide results from …** on a release keeps someone out of your search results (in this
browser), for example after a fake or a failed download.
